PCP Mis-Selling Explained

Personal Contract Purchase (PCP) mis-selling involves the improper sale of car finance deals, often without full disclosure of terms or suitability for the buyer.

Part of the complete guide: Car finance mis-selling explained →

Understanding PCP Mis-Selling

PCP mis-selling occurs when a car finance deal is sold without proper explanation of the terms, costs, or suitability for the buyer.

This can lead to financial difficulties and unexpected costs for the consumer.

Signs of PCP Mis-Selling

Common signs include lack of clarity on terms, undisclosed costs, and deals that do not match the buyer's financial situation.

If you feel pressured into a deal or were not given full information, you might have been mis-sold.

Consequences of Mis-Selling

Mis-selling can result in financial loss, unexpected expenses, and being tied to an unsuitable contract.

It is important to understand your rights and options if you suspect mis-selling.

Steps to Take if Mis-Sold

If you believe you were mis-sold, contact the Financial Ombudsman Service, seek legal advice, or discuss with the lender.

Claiming Compensation

You may be entitled to compensation if you can prove that you were mis-sold a PCP deal.

Gather all relevant documents and seek professional advice to pursue a claim.
